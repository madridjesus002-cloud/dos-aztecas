Dos Aztecas recipe

Carne Asada
Steak Tacos

Smoky, citrusy steak tucked into warm homemade nixtamal tortillas and finished with onion, cilantro, green salsa, and lime.

Prep15 minMarinate30 minCook10 minServes4
8 tacos · easy weeknight dinner

What you’ll need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb skirt or flank steak
  • ½ cup Dos Aztecas Carne Asada Marinade
  • 8 Dos Aztecas Homemade Nixtamal Tortillas
  • ½ small white onion, finely diced
  • ½ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Dos Aztecas Green Salsa, for serving
  • Salt, if needed

Let’s cook

Method

  1. 01

    Marinate the steak

    Place the steak and Carne Asada Marinade in a covered dish or food-safe bag. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes and up to 4 hours.

  2. 02

    Get a good sear

    Heat a grill or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Remove excess marinade, then cook the steak for about 4–5 minutes per side, or until it reaches 145°F. Rest for at least 3 minutes.

  3. 03

    Slice against the grain

    Cut the rested steak into thin strips against the grain so every bite stays tender.

  4. 04

    Warm the tortillas

    Warm each tortilla in a dry skillet for 20–30 seconds per side. Stack them in a clean kitchen towel to keep them soft.

  5. 05

    Build the tacos

    Divide the steak among the warm tortillas. Top with onion, cilantro, green salsa, and a squeeze of fresh lime.

Make it yours: Add sliced avocado, crumbled queso fresco, or charred scallions for an extra layer of flavor.
